# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. Everyone who participates — in issues, pull requests, discussions, or any other project space — is expected to follow it.

## In short

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Critique ideas and skills, not people.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Don't publish anyone's private information, including customer or deal data from your own work.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers by opening a [private security advisory](https://github.com/pratsy/B2B_agent_skills/security/advisories/new) (it is visible only to maintainers) or by contacting a maintainer directly through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content and restrict participation for anyone who violates this code, following the [Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
